Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. This policy explains what Hydra Neck collects, why, and your rights. Because the service is operated from Brazil, Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) applies; if you live in the EU, the UK or another place with similar laws (such as the GDPR), you have the rights those laws give you as well.

1. Who is responsible

Hydra Neck is operated by Samuel Ferreira da Silva, an individual based in Brazil, who is the controller of the data about customers described below and the person to contact about privacy (see section 8).

2. What we collect

About you, the customer

  • Your Telegram user ID and @username.
  • Your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, subscription status and renewal date.
  • Messages you send to the bot through /support.

About the groups you connect

  • Group IDs and titles, forum topic names, and which groups are paired as source and backup.
  • Your settings: filter mode, quarantine delay, paused status.
  • The Telegram user IDs of the members you mark as trusted.
  • Message identifiers only: the ID of each source message, the ID of its copy, album and reply links, and the copy status. These let us replicate edits and replies and avoid duplicates.

What we don't collect: we don't download or store the text, photos, videos or files of messages — Telegram copies them directly between groups. We never see your card details; Stripe collects your card, name, e-mail and billing country under its own privacy policy, and shares with us only what is listed above.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service you subscribed to — pairing groups, filtering and copying messages, and keeping copies in sync (legal basis: performance of a contract).
  • To bill you and manage your subscription (performance of a contract; legal obligations for tax and accounting records).
  • To prevent abuse and keep the bot running — investigating reports, enforcing the Terms of Service, and keeping short technical logs (legitimate interest).
  • To answer your requests sent through /support.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you.

4. Your group members' data

For the members of the groups you connect, you decide what is mirrored and to which groups; we process their identifiers only on your instructions and to run the service. Members who want their posts removed from a backup should contact the group's administrators; we will help you act on those requests.

5. Who we share it with

  • Telegram, which operates the platform and performs the copies.
  • Stripe, our payment processor.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to report illegal content such as child sexual abuse material.

Our database runs on a server we control in Brazil. Telegram and Stripe process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards; using them is necessary to provide the service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Customer, group and message-identifier data: while you have an account with us. If you ask us to delete it, we do so within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.
  • Billing records held by Stripe: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.
  • Technical logs: rotated automatically and kept only for a short time.
  • Records linked to a serious abuse report: as long as needed to deal with it or as required by law.

7. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), the GDPR and similar laws, you can ask us to confirm whether we process your data, give you access to it, correct it, delete it, port it, tell you who we share it with, and review a consent you gave, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest. You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) or to the authority where you live. Deleting your data ends the service for the groups involved.

8. Contact

Send /support followed by your request to @HydraNeckBot. We reply through the bot, normally within 15 days.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you through the bot before the change takes effect; the date at the top shows the current version.
