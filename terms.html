🐍 Hydra Neck

Terms of use

Draft, under review. Last updated: November 22, 2026.

1. The service

Hydra Neck is a Telegram bot (@HydraNeckBot) that, at the customer's request, copies messages from a source group to backup groups the customer chooses. Copies are made by Telegram itself; Hydra Neck does not store photos, videos or files.

2. Responsibility for content

Customers are solely responsible for the groups they manage and for the content that is mirrored. Using the service with illegal content is forbidden, including child sexual abuse or exploitation material, piracy, scams, or anything that violates Telegram's Terms of Service. Such use leads to immediate cancellation without refund, and to reporting to the authorities where the law requires it.

3. Data we keep

We keep the customer's Telegram ID, the IDs of paired groups, the list of trusted members and the IDs of copied messages, only to operate the service. Payment data stays with Stripe.

4. Subscription and cancellation

The subscription costs US$ 20 per month, charged to a card through Stripe, and renews automatically. Customers can cancel at any time via the /account command; the service stays active until the end of the paid period. If a payment fails, backups pause after 3 days.

5. Limitations

The service depends on Telegram and Stripe. We do not guarantee that a group will never be removed, nor that messages sent before the bot joined, or deleted during quarantine, will be copied.

6. Reporting abuse

To report misuse, message @HydraNeckBot.
