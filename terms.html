2. The service

Hydra Neck is a Telegram bot that keeps backup copies of Telegram groups you manage. Once you add it as an administrator to a source group and to one or more backup groups, it can:

  • copy new posts from the source group to the backup groups — by default only posts from people you mark as trusted, optionally after a quarantine delay, so that posts deleted during the delay are never copied;
  • copy existing media from a group's history into another group (/clone);
  • copy the group's name, description, photo and forum topics to the backup.

Copies are made by Telegram itself through its Bot API. Hydra Neck does not download or store the text, photos, videos or files of your messages — only the identifiers needed to operate (see the Privacy Policy). A subscription covers up to 5 backup groups.
